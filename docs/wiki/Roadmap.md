# Roadmap

Planned, roughly in order. Nothing here is promised by a date.

- [ ] **Game sound without a virtual cable** — capture WASAPI loopback directly.
- [ ] **Pre-roll buffer** — keep the last 15–30 s so the first hit of a fight is always in the clip.
- [ ] **More metadata** — zone, enemy name/class, win or death, in file names, the clips panel and MP4 tags (v1.6).
- [ ] **Kill / death chapter markers** inside clips.
- [ ] **Daily highlight reel** — stitch the day's clips without re-encoding.
- [ ] **Thumbnails** in the clips panel; experimental low-fps in-game preview.
- [ ] **Autostart** with Windows or WoW, tray icon.
- [ ] **Code-signed EXE** and Microsoft Store listing.
- [ ] **More clients** tested and listed on CurseForge.

Ideas and votes: [issues](https://github.com/Ionity-Global/wrc-record-combat-ionity/issues).
